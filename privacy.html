Privacy

Updated November 24, 2026. This student-maintained website shares Interlake swim and dive team information. Questions or requests to remove a post or photo can be sent to max.interlake.swim@gmail.com.

Reading team updates

You do not need an account to read published updates. Vercel hosts the website and processes requests needed to deliver its pages and images, which can include IP addresses and browser information.

Editor accounts and posts

Only approved editors can sign in to write updates. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in and password resets. The website uses the editor’s email address, sign-in method, verification status, and account identifier to check posting permission. Coach Eberth can use his password without email verification. Editor sign-in state is stored in the browser. Post text, dates, publication status, and author identifiers are stored in Google Cloud Firestore.

Photos and Google Drive access

The Interlake Swim Photos integration uses a dedicated team Google account to store uploaded photos. It requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, allowing access to files created by the app or explicitly shared with it. Visitors and editors do not need to connect their personal Google Drive accounts.

Uploaded photos are resized and stored in the team’s Drive folder. The website’s server reads them from that folder to display them. Anyone with an uploaded photo’s website URL can view it, even if its post is still a draft. Editors should upload only photos suitable for public sharing.

Removing a photo from a post or unpublishing a post does not delete the stored image. Contact the team site maintainer at the address above to request removal. Published content and uploaded images remain until removed; copies may temporarily remain in browser or hosting caches.

Service providers and access

The website uses Google services for authentication, post storage, and photo storage, and Vercel for hosting and serving photos. The Drive connection credentials are stored as server-side secrets in Vercel. They are not provided to website visitors. The dedicated Google account owner can revoke the app’s Drive access in Google Account settings; doing so stops photo uploads and retrieval through that connection.
