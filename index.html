HEAD COACH

Coach Eberth Rivera Gomez

Interlake boys’ swim & dive · 2026–27

Coach Eberth Rivera Gomez brings more than 30 years of experience in athletic coaching and competitive sports to Interlake High School. His experience spans both the United States and Colombia. Coach Eberth worked alongside the previous head coach for multiple seasons, supporting Interlake swimmers as they competed at the state championships. This season, he serves as head coach, supporting swimmers at different stages of their development, from those taking their first competitive laps to experienced athletes working to refine their skills.

Interlake’s boys’ swim and dive team has competed at the Washington state championships across multiple seasons. The team was also named WIAA 3A Boys Swim and Dive Academic State Champion in 2024 and 2025, recognizing its achievements in the classroom.

His coaching approach emphasizes individual growth, discipline, and confidence. Coach Eberth values open communication and mutual respect, and works with swimmers and their families to build a supportive team environment. He is committed to listening to his athletes, recognizing their individual strengths, and helping each swimmer develop a lasting appreciation for the sport.

More about Coach Eberth

Every swimmer matters. Coach Eberth believes each athlete has unique potential, whether they are taking their first competitive laps or refining advanced skills. He meets swimmers where they are and helps them build confidence, discipline, and a lasting love of the sport.

His coaching philosophy centers on open communication, mutual respect, and individual growth. He is committed to listening closely, working alongside families, and creating a supportive team where every athlete feels valued, heard, and empowered to thrive.

Originally from Bogotá, Colombia, Coach Eberth moved to the United States about 14 years ago. Beyond swimming, he enjoys coaching and playing soccer, camping, and spending time outdoors with his family.

Jan 28 Home Meet

We need enough parents to volunteer for the required roles. If we cannot fill them, we cannot host the meet.

We hosted four home meets last season. This season we have only one. Our final dual meet on Jan. 28 is also Senior Night. We hope every swimmer and their parents can attend to celebrate our seniors and support the team.

To run the meet, we need 12 timers (two per lane), 2 backup timers, 1 runner, 1 starter/announcer, 1 stroke and turn official, 1 scoreboard operator, and 1 computer operator. These roles are based on last season’s checklist; we’ll confirm final assignments with the meet staff.

Please choose a role on the Jan. 28 home-meet volunteer sign-up ↗. For questions, contact parent coordinator Yingde Tang. New volunteers are welcome; the short officials video below is a good introduction.

KingCo and District Meets

We need enough parent volunteers for the team to participate. Without them, we cannot attend these postseason meets.
