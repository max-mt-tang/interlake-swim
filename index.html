HEAD COACH

Coach Eberth Rivera

Interlake boys’ swim & dive · 2026–27

Coach Eberth Rivera brings more than 30 years of experience in athletic coaching and competitive sports to Interlake High School. His experience spans both the United States and Colombia. Coach Eberth worked alongside the previous head coach for multiple seasons, supporting Interlake swimmers as they competed at the state championships. This season, he serves as head coach, supporting swimmers at different stages of their development, from those taking their first competitive laps to experienced athletes working to refine their skills.

Interlake’s boys’ swim and dive team has competed at the Washington state championships across multiple seasons, including 2024 and 2025. The team was also named WIAA 3A Boys Swim and Dive Academic State Champion in 2024 and 2025, recognizing its achievements in the classroom.

His coaching approach emphasizes individual growth, discipline, and confidence. Coach Eberth values open communication and mutual respect, and works with swimmers and their families to build a supportive team environment. He is committed to listening to his athletes, recognizing their individual strengths, and helping each swimmer develop a lasting appreciation for the sport.

More about Coach Eberth

Every swimmer matters. Coach Eberth believes each athlete has unique potential, whether they are taking their first competitive laps or refining advanced skills. He meets swimmers where they are and helps them build confidence, discipline, and a lasting love of the sport.

His coaching philosophy centers on open communication, mutual respect, and individual growth. He is committed to listening closely, working alongside families, and creating a supportive team where every athlete feels valued, heard, and empowered to thrive.

Originally from Bogotá, Colombia, Coach Eberth moved to the United States about 14 years ago. Beyond swimming, he enjoys coaching and playing soccer, camping, and spending time outdoors with his family.
